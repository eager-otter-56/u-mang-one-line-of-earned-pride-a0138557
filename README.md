# One Line of Earned Pride

A single line of quiet, earned pride: after a stroke takes his left hand,
a grandfather spends eleven months relearning a knot one-handed at his
kitchen table — and one morning ties his granddaughter's shoelaces without
looking down, so smoothly that she never knows it was hard.

`main.py` writes the line to `entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`
(default `/output`).
